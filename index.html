        02 / Legal and aftercare

        Know what happens after an incident.

        Self-defense law varies by jurisdiction and facts. A surviving family member may bring a civil claim even when criminal charges are not filed. Speak with a licensed attorney.

        01

        Do not give a detailed statement from memory.

        Request medical care and ask for a lawyer. Be truthful, identify yourself, and follow lawful instructions.

        02

        Preserve evidence without manipulating it.

        Save messages, note witnesses, and avoid posting details publicly. Do not delete or edit relevant material.

        03

        Use official legal resources.

        03 / Safety basics

        Small habits, practiced often.

        01

        Emergency communication

        Keep your phone charged, location-sharing intentional, and emergency contacts written down.

        02

        Medical readiness

        Take CPR and bleeding-control training. Know where the closest staffed medical facility is.

        03

        Secure storage

        Any weapon in a home must be stored according to local law, inaccessible to unauthorized people and children. Training and safe handling come first.

        04

        De-escalation

        Use a calm voice, create space, avoid ego contests, and leave when a safe exit exists.
